Who We Are

Youths Wey Love Jesus (YWLJ) exists to inspire and equip young people to know Jesus, grow in faith and make Him known. We believe young people can serve God meaningfully today and become positive influences in their schools, churches and communities.

Our Foundation

Jesus Christ is at the centre of our identity, faith and service.

Our Commitment

Prayer, the Word, fellowship and practical Christian living.

Our Motto

Endeavour for Christ — living out our faith with purpose and dedication.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision

To see a generation of young people passionately devoted to Jesus Christ and positively impacting their world.

Mission

To reach, teach, encourage and equip youths through school outreach, evangelism, prayer gatherings, discipleship and youth-centred Christian programmes.

Our Mandate

To make Christ known, strengthen young believers and encourage young people to live purposeful lives rooted in the Gospel.

What We Do

Our activities build faith, create meaningful fellowship and take the message of Christ beyond our gatherings.

School Outreach

Sharing the Gospel and encouraging Christian growth in schools and learning communities.

Prayer & Intercession

Gathering young people to pray for one another, the Church and the nation.

Discipleship & Teaching

Studying Scripture and helping young believers build a strong foundation in Christ.

Worship & Fellowship

Opportunities for worship, unity, encouragement and spiritual development.

Youth Leadership

Encouraging responsibility, service, integrity and purposeful Christian leadership.

Community Evangelism

Taking the message of hope and salvation into our communities.

Executive Leadership

The team entrusted with guiding the vision, coordinating the work and serving the YWLJ community.
