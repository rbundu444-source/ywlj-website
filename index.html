Our identity

More than a name.
A commitment to Christ.

Youths Wey Love Jesus (YWLJ) is a Christ-centred youth movement that exists to inspire and equip young people to know Jesus, grow in faith and make Him known.

Our motto

“Endeavour for Christ.”

Not merely in words, but in character, devotion, service and the daily decision to follow Jesus.

Our desire: to see young people strengthened in the faith and equipped to be lights in their schools, churches and communities.

Where we are going

Vision, Mission & Mandate

Our Vision

To see a generation of young people passionately devoted to Jesus Christ and positively impacting their world.

Our Mission

To reach, teach, encourage and equip youths through evangelism, prayer, discipleship, fellowship and youth-centred Christian programmes.

Our Mandate

To make Christ known, strengthen young believers and encourage purposeful lives rooted in the Gospel.

Our Values

Christlikeness, prayer, the Word of God, unity, integrity, service, excellence and love.

Faith expressed through action

Our Programmes & Outreach

We create opportunities for young people to grow spiritually, build meaningful relationships and share the hope of Jesus Christ.

01 / OUTREACH

School Outreach

Sharing the Gospel and encouraging Christian growth in schools and learning communities.

02 / PRAYER

Prayer & Intercession

Gathering young people to pray for one another, the Church, our communities and the nation.

03 / DISCIPLESHIP

Teaching the Word

Studying Scripture and helping young believers build a strong foundation in Christ.

04 / WORSHIP

Worship & Fellowship

Creating room for worship, unity, encouragement and spiritual development.

05 / LEADERSHIP

Youth Leadership

Encouraging responsibility, integrity, service and purposeful Christian leadership.

06 / COMMUNITY

Community Evangelism

Taking the message of hope and salvation beyond our gatherings and into our communities.
